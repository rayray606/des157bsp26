The Future of Korean Pastor's Kids

An interactive project exploring faith, family expectations, church culture, and mental health.

Current Reality

Korean pastor's kids often grow up with high expectations from family, church members, and their community. They may feel pressure to be role models, hide struggles, and represent their parents' ministry in a positive way.

Future Without Change

If mental health continues to be ignored, pastor's kids may feel isolated, misunderstood, or disconnected from church communities. Silence around mental health can create burnout and emotional pressure.

Future With Support

A healthier future could include open conversations, counseling resources, support groups, and stronger boundaries for pastor families. Churches could become places where mental health is supported instead of hidden.

Reflection

What kind of future should faith communities create?
