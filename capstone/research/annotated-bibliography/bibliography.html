Annotated Bibliography

Source 1: Korean Pastors’ Perspectives on Mental Health

Citation: Cho, Gloria Hyun Young. Korean Pastors’ Perspectives on the Mental Health and Related Needs of the Korean-Canadian Community. University of British Columbia, 2024.

This thesis explores Korean pastors' perspectives on mental health within Korean-Canadian communities. The author found that stigma, cultural expectations, and limited mental health education often prevent people from seeking help. The study also highlights the church's role as both a source of support and a place where mental health challenges may be misunderstood. This source is useful for my project because it directly relates to Korean church culture and mental health. I concluded that churches can play an important role in reducing stigma and supporting the well-being of pastor's kids and other congregation members.

Source 2: Springer Article

Citation: Yoo, Nari, et al. “Korean American Church Leaders as Mental Health Gatekeepers in the USA: A Needs Assessment of Readiness, Barriers, and Referrals - Journal of Religion and Health.” SpringerLink, Springer US, 12 Mar. 2026, link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s10943-026-02589-3.

This article examines the relationship between religion, mental health, and Christian communities. The authors discuss how faith can provide emotional support while also creating challenges when mental health struggles are viewed as spiritual weaknesses. The research is useful because it helps explain how religious beliefs can influence attitudes toward counseling and mental health treatment. As a peer-reviewed academic source, it adds credibility to my project. I concluded that understanding both the positive and negative impacts of religion on mental health is important when discussing the experiences of pastor's kids.

Source 3: Erasing Shame Podcast

Citation: “Pastor’s Kids Unlearning Legalism (S7E05) by Erasing Shame.” Spotify for Creators, podcasters.spotify.com/pod/show/erasingshame/episodes/Pastors-Kids-Unlearning-Legalism-s7e05-e2ko600. Accessed 13 May 2026.

This podcast focuses on the experiences of pastor's kids and the pressure they often face within church environments. The speakers discuss topics such as perfectionism, identity, faith, and the impact of strict religious expectations on mental health. This source is useful because it provides personal perspectives and lived experiences that are not always found in academic research. It helps me better understand the emotional challenges that some pastor's kids experience. I concluded that personal stories can make mental health topics more relatable and impactful for audiences.

Source 4: SOLA Network Interview

Citation: Aaron Lee, Danny Kwon. “Teenagers and Mental Health: An Interview with Danny Kwon and Monica Kim.” SOLA Network, 16 Nov. 2025, sola.network/article/teenagers-mental-health-interview-danny-kwon-monica-kim/.

This interview discusses mental health concerns among teenagers and the importance of addressing these issues within Christian communities. The speakers emphasize the need for open conversations, support systems, and reducing stigma around seeking help. This source is relevant because many pastor's kids experience mental health challenges during adolescence and young adulthood. The interview provides practical insights into how churches can better support young people. I concluded that increasing mental health awareness within faith communities can create healthier and more supportive environments for future generations.
